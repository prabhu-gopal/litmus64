# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**In short:** be decent. Assume competence and good faith. Critique work, never people. Technical
disagreement is welcome and is how this project gets better; contempt is not.

One project-specific note, because it matters here: **reporting that the tool was wrong is a
contribution, not an attack.** Anyone who makes a person feel unwelcome for showing us a false
positive has misunderstood what we are building.

Report concerns to `conduct@litmus64.com`. Reports are handled confidentially. Enforcement follows the
Covenant's escalation ladder, and enforcement decisions are recorded.
